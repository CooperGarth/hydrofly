# Super Pit reference level

HydroFly uses a **Super Pit-inspired synthetic level**, not a calibrated KCGM model.

| Reference | Value used | Scope |
|---|---:|---|
| Planned final shell length | 3,780 m | 2022 closure model |
| Planned final shell width | 1,610 m | 2022 closure model |
| Average crest | 360 m AHD | Approximately 351–390 m AHD |
| Final floor | −390 m AHD | 2022 closure model |
| Depth from average crest | 750 m | Planned shell, not current measured depth |
| Mine-life context | 2034+ | Planning horizon, not a fixed closure date |

Source: [KCGM closure water balance and pit lake geochemistry, 1 August 2022, p.13](https://www.superpit.com.au/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/App-U-KCGM-Pit-Lake.pdf). That study describes a final void of about 479 ha. The renderer uses a simplified ellipse and terraces matching the length, width and elevation span; it is not a surveyed outline and does not reproduce the published area.

Northern Star's [Fimiston South approval announcement](https://www.superpit.com.au/fimistonsouth/) gives 2034; its [September 2025 investor presentation](https://announcements.asx.com.au/asxpdf/20250915/pdf/06p7b7fvr21868.pdf) describes extension past 2034. This is a mine-life reference, not the game's hydraulic simulation duration. Research checked 13 September 2026.

## Synthetic hydraulic assumptions

Ten wells surround the rim. The floor control ellipse has radii 800 and 100 m; these dimensions are invented. Initial piezometric head is −365 m AHD, representing an already partly dewatered scenario. The target is −395 m AHD. K=0.2 m/day, b=200 m, S=0.001, roof=−900 m AHD and a 365-day constant-rate assessment are synthetic. The confined aquifer lies beneath the pit; the pit is a control footprint, not an excavation boundary. This is a pressure-head experiment, not a simulation of a 750 m falling water table.

The [March 2024 Fimiston South environmental review, p.158](https://www.superpit.com.au/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/Fimiston-South-ERD-v4-20240311-Print-version-v2.pdf) describes very low wall seepage, a local cone of depression and water held in historic voids. A homogeneous radial-well model does not capture that system. No hydraulic parameter is inferred from pit dimensions. No claim of KCGM endorsement or predictive dewatering accuracy is made.
