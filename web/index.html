How to play

Your goal: keep groundwater at least 5 m below the stepped mine floor while reducing your selected pumping impact.

  1. Set the challenge. Open Mine plan & strategy, edit the bores and year-end floors, then Create / resize. For a fresh challenge, click Start from uniform pumping—the initial demo is already conventionally fitted.
  2. Choose an objective. Pumping cost minimises synthetic operating cost; Total pumping rate minimises pumped volume; Excess drawdown reduces unnecessary lowering below the pit target.
  3. Train fly. Watch the brain updates and episode rewards. The graph retains the best plan, including guided experiments. Pumping may need to increase first to meet the target.
  4. Keep learning. Continue training adds experiments. Select Train until paused for a longer run. Pause finishes the current calculation before stopping.
  5. Watch the result. Run full simulation plays the plan from day 0 to its end. Read the maximum drawdown, 1 m contour reach, pumped volume and cost as they accumulate. Export Excel saves the current plan and freshly calculated results. Edit its Settings and Mine plan sheets, then Import Excel to load it back (clears previous learning).

Useful controls: YEAR buttons select the rates to edit. All off and Equalise affect that year. Evaluate refreshes results after edits. Calculate scenario benchmark compares with a conventional solution; Fit water level applies a conventional drawdown fit and does not train the fly.

Reading the graph: the dashed line is floor minus 5 m. Orange water-level samples miss the target. Maximum drawdown includes bore locations; contour reach is measured from the pit centre. A ≥ distance is a lower bound. Peak drawdown and reach are monitoring metrics, not separate optimiser objectives.

Explore: drag to orbit, scroll/pinch to zoom, and Expand the world. To calculate a larger area, change Aquifer properties → Modelled half-width and evaluate again.

If a run cannot proceed: check the elapsed message and any feasibility error. More training cannot solve an impossible borefield. The initial water level must already be 5 m below the initial floor. Reloading clears the learning session; Export preserves a record.

Synthetic educational model. Targets are checked at monthly samples, not continuously. Brain flashes illustrate controller activity. Costs are synthetic operating costs only.

Methods, limitations and inspiration

Actual timflow transient results drive the head surface. Bore rates are constant within each 365-day year; recovery and previous years carry forward. The floor footprint is fixed while its elevation changes. This is not a moving excavation boundary. Monthly compliance does not establish continuous safety.

The 2022 planned Super Pit shell supplies the 3.78 × 1.61 km outline and 750 m reference depth. The coloured geology is an illustrative interpretation of the regional volcanic, sedimentary and Golden Mile Dolerite setting; it does not assign separate hydraulic properties. The analytical aquifer remains homogeneous and confined.

Linear Q-learning uses normalised α=0.05, γ=0.95, epsilon-greedy exploration and decaying strategy guidance. Training rewards, success and action values are computed, not scripted animations. Training includes teacher guidance and can fail to find a feasible plan. Validation on this plan is not evidence of generalisation.
