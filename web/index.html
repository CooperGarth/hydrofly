Methods, limitations and inspiration

Actual timflow transient results drive the head surface. Bore rates are constant within each 365-day year; recovery and previous years carry forward. The floor footprint is fixed while its elevation changes. This is not a moving excavation boundary. Quarterly compliance does not establish continuous safety.

The 2022 planned Super Pit shell supplies the 3.78 × 1.61 km outline and 750 m reference depth. The coloured geology is an illustrative interpretation of the regional volcanic, sedimentary and Golden Mile Dolerite setting; it does not assign separate hydraulic properties. The analytical aquifer remains homogeneous and confined.

Q-learning uses α=0.2, γ=0.95, epsilon-greedy exploration and decaying strategy guidance. Training rewards, success and action values are computed, not scripted animations. The learned policy can fail and is evaluated without teacher assistance. Validation on this plan is not evidence of generalisation.
